# Submission text (Cycle III, category: Earn)

**Name:** Payday
**Tagline:** Turn your NIM into a paycheck.

## Description (under 250 words)

Payday turns NIM staking into something everyone already understands: a paycheck.

Most NIM holders never stake because "APY", "delegation" and "retire stake" sound like homework. Payday asks one
question: how much NIM should work for you? Type an amount and you instantly see your weekly, monthly and yearly
pay in NIM and your own currency. Tap once and Nimiq Pay stakes it with a validator Payday picked for you
(uptime, fee, and whether rewards compound), or pick your own from a ranked list.

After that, Payday is your payslip: a big weekly paycheck number, a live "earned today" ticker, what the chain
says you've actually been paid so far, and a paycheck goal ("I want 100 NIM a week") that tells you exactly how
much more to stake. Withdrawing is a clear three-step card with a real countdown to the release block, so you
always know when your NIM comes home. You can also switch validators without unstaking, and share a
"My NIM pays me X every week" card that opens Payday in Nimiq Pay.

Who it's for: anyone holding NIM in Nimiq Pay who wants it to earn, especially people new to staking.

Nimiq integration: every action is a native Nimiq Pay transaction through the Mini App SDK
(sendNewStaker, sendStake, sendSetActiveStake, sendRetireStake, sendRemoveStake, sendUpdateStaker), with full
error handling. Rates are live: today's network emission divided by all staked NIM, minus fees. No backend, no
custody. Works on mainnet and testnet.
